## Looking for co-maintainers

lit-material has one maintainer today. We're looking for a second, and that matters to teams adopting it:
it means reviews and releases don't stall when one person is away.

A co-maintainer reviews and merges pull requests, triages issues, and can cut releases. If you've contributed
a few non-trivial changes and want to help, say so in an
[issue](https://github.com/bohdaq/lit-material/issues).

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are added by the existing maintainers, after a track record of reviewed contributions. A
maintainer who has been inactive for 12 months moves to an "emeritus" list. Decisions follow
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#decisions): lazy consensus in the pull request or RFC, with the lead
maintainer deciding when there isn't one.
