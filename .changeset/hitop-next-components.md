Three new packages. `@lit-material/command-palette`: a modal ⌘K palette on the native `<dialog>` with fuzzy matching and highlighted letters, sections, prefixes that limit to a section, the ARIA combobox pattern, and `select`, `close` and cancelable `tab` events; it binds no global keys. `@lit-material/timing-waterfall`: where a request's time went (DNS, connect, waiting, download) as one stacked bar and a legend, from a `timings` object or your own phases. `@lit-material/method-tag`: an HTTP or API method as a monospace label coloured by method. Translations for their labels in every catalog.
