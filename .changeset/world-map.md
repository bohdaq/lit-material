New `@lit-material/world-map`: countries shaded by a value per country (choropleth) on a square-root scale, with a legend, keyboard reading highest first, optional selection (`change`), countries too small to draw listed under the map, and a screen-reader table. Shapes from Natural Earth 1:110m (public domain), about 12 kB gzipped.
