Tabs for open documents: `scrollable` on the tabs (tabs keep their width, the row scrolls, the selected tab is kept in view; an `end` slot for a New tab button), `closable` on a tab (a × that fires a cancelable `close`, also from Delete and a middle click) and `dirty` (a dot and "Unsaved changes" in its description). The indicator now follows its tab while the row scrolls.
